SHINSEI VILLAGE

VISION

Shinsei Village is a unique collection of 1,200 demigod monkeys, each crafted with distinct traits, powerful abilities, and rich lore. Inspired by ancient legends and mystical adventures, every character represents a guardian of the village, bringing together art, storytelling, and community in one immersive experience.

Every stage of the Shinsei Village journey is designed to unlock new adventures for its holders. From exclusive community events and collectible rewards to future expansions and interactive experiences, the project aims to create a living world where members can explore, connect, and become part of an evolving story.
